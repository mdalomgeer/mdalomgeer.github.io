I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Information Systems at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County, advised by Dr. Tera L. Reynolds in the CARE Research Lab. I build and evaluate large language model systems for patients' health questions, and study which judgments an automated judge can make and which need a clinician or the patient. Our team's question-answering pipeline over patient records placed first of 13 teams in question interpretation at the ArchEHR-QA 2026 shared task, and I received the Doctorate Student Award at UMBC COEIT Research Day (2026).
